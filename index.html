About untangled

Untangled is a weekly podcast profiling one Asia-based company per episode — the kind of business stories that haven't been told yet in English-language media. Each episode is paired with a full research dossier published here, so listeners can dig into the data behind the conversation.

The format: 45-60 minute audio episodes featuring conversations with operators, investors, and analysts who actually understand the company being profiled. The website acts as the show notes — but with depth. Every claim made on the air is traceable to data in the dossier.

Why "untangled"

Most coverage of Asian companies in Western media reduces them to a single narrative — usually "China copying the West" or "the next Alibaba." The reality is messier and more interesting. Pulling on one thread (an outbound licensing deal, a regulatory approval, a quiet acquisition) almost always reveals a much larger story underneath. The show is about following those threads.

Why pharma for Episode 1

Chinese biopharma is undergoing the most consequential business transformation in Asia right now. In 2025, Chinese-origin assets accounted for over $135B in outbound licensing deals with Western pharma — a 161% year-over-year increase. Innovent sits at the center of this shift, and the Takeda $11.4B deal in October 2025 made the company impossible to ignore. The full dossier explains why.
